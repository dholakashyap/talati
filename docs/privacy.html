Privacy Policy

Talati Exam Prep (com.dhola.talati) by Dhola Infotech · Last updated 16 October 2026

Talati Exam Prep is a free app to prepare for the Gujarat Panchayat Service Selection Board (GPSSB) Talati cum Mantri exam. It is not an official app of GPSSB or the Government of Gujarat.

No account, no personal data from you

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email or location. The nickname you may type stays on your phone.

Your progress stays on your phone

Answers, scores, streaks, bookmarks and settings are stored only on your device. Android's own backup may copy them to your Google account if you have backup turned on; that is controlled by you in Android settings. The in-app "Backup code" is a text you copy yourself; we never receive it.

Content downloads

The app downloads current-affairs content from a public file on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). These requests send no personal information; like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address. Links to official websites open in your browser.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob and Google Ad Manager (including our partner Ad.Plus) and from CAS.ai mediation (AppLovin, Kidoz, Prado). These ad providers may collect and use device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model and app usage to show and measure ads, as described in their own policies:

  • Google: how ads work and Google Privacy Policy
  • CAS.ai Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin Privacy Policy

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time from Profile → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

The app is meant for adult exam candidates and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
